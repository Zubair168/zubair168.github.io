About the app

Restaurant order management without the complexity.

Everything a waiter needs for everyday order taking, organized into a focused mobile workflow.

Waiter: Order Taking App helps restaurant staff take customer orders accurately, manage table-wise orders, review totals and print professional receipts from an Android device.

The app is intentionally simple. There is no complicated POS setup and no login requirement. Staff can install the app and start managing orders immediately.

It is designed to work offline, making it practical for restaurants and food businesses that need a dependable local ordering workflow.
